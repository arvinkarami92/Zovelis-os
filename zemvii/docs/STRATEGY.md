# Zemvii strategy

**Zemvii is the social network that works for you, not on you.**
Your people first, a feed you control, real humans, a finish line instead of an
endless scroll, and a fair deal for creators.

Tagline: **Your people. Your feed. Your rules.**

This document records what the big platforms are missing in 2026, what Zemvii
offers instead, and how a newcomer can realistically win. Sources are listed at
the end; figures from vendor blogs are marked as such.

---

## 1. What the giants are missing (2026)

### 1.1 You don't control your feed

* Instagram's most common complaint in app-store reviews is that the algorithm
  hides posts from accounts people follow and pushes Reels ahead of photos.
  Facebook's reviews complain that the feed shows irrelevant content [1].
* Feeds became "entertainment media": recommended strangers instead of
  friends, with no explanation of why a post appears and only buried,
  temporary escape hatches (a chronological view you have to keep re-selecting).
* Custom feeds are the one idea from smaller networks that is spreading
  (Bluesky's feeds, Threads rolling out custom feeds) [9], which shows demand
  for control.

### 1.2 Built to be compulsive

* 45% of US teens say they spend too much time on social media, up from 27%
  in 2023 (Pew) [6].
* In 2026 a Los Angeles jury found Meta and YouTube negligent in the design of
  their platforms in a social-media-addiction case ($3M damages plus $3M
  recommended punitive). Meta has appealed; thousands of similar cases from
  parents, school districts and states are waiting [5].
* Australia has banned under-16s from holding accounts since 10 December
  2025 (fines up to A$49.5M); France, Denmark, the European Parliament and
  others are moving the same way [4].
* Infinite scroll, autoplay, streaks and notification bait are the business
  model, so the incumbents cannot remove them without hurting revenue.

### 1.3 AI slop and fake people

* "Slop" mentions more than tripled in 2025 and 82% of the sentiment is
  negative (Brandwatch) [2]. Estimates of AI-generated content in feeds range
  from 20–40% across social media up to ~70% of images on Facebook (single
  source, treat with caution) [2].
* Platform labelling is reactive and error-prone: TikTok labelled a human-made
  collage as "AI-generated" and quietly removed the label [2]. Artists now tag
  their own posts "not AI" just to prove they are human [2].

### 1.4 Your friends left the feed

* Ordinary people stopped posting publicly. Instagram's own leadership says
  growth is in Stories and DMs. Sharing moved to Close Friends lists, group
  chats and alternate accounts [7].
* Group chats, however, have no shared memory, no planning tools and no way
  to meet up. Meanwhile Instagram removed end-to-end encrypted DMs on
  8 May 2026 [7], even as DMs became where personal sharing happens.

### 1.5 Creators are underpaid and replaceable

* Earnings are extremely concentrated; vendor reports say the top 10% take
  most of the payment volume and over half of creators earn under $15k a
  year [8].
* Platform payouts are volatile and shrinking (TikTok's creator fund pay
  declined; ad-share schemes pay less) [1][8]. Creators cannot reliably reach
  their own followers without paying or chasing trends.

### 1.6 Black-box moderation and lockouts

* Facebook's top review complaint: accounts disabled or hacked with **no
  recovery path**; also Marketplace scams [1].
* Instagram and TikTok: suspensions and removals without explanation [1].
  There is rarely a human to talk to and rarely a real appeal.

### 1.7 Privacy as the price of admission

* Surveillance advertising funds all three. TikTok's US entity (the TikTok
  USDS Joint Venture, established 22 January 2026) is retraining its
  recommendation algorithm on US data under new owners [3], so users again
  face new terms and an algorithm in flux.

## 3. Zemvii's pillars

1. **Your people first.** The Friends feed shows everything from people you
   follow, newest first, and ends. Circles let every post go to exactly the
   right people (Close friends, Family, Team…).
2. **Your feed, your rules.** Feed Dials tune the Mix feed in plain language;
   presets ("Balanced", "Just my people", "Explorer", "Calm Sunday") make it
   one tap. Every post has a "Why am I seeing this?" explanation generated by
   the same code that ranked it.
3. **Real humans, honest media.** Every post carries an origin label: Human
   made, AI‑assisted or AI‑generated. People can hide AI content entirely.
   "Verified human" badges come from a privacy-preserving check (no ID is
   stored). Undisclosed AI is a reportable offence.
4. **A finish line.** No infinite scroll by default: "You're all caught up",
   Clips arrive in sets of eight with a breathing pause, a daily time budget
   with a gentle check-in, quiet hours, batched notification digests, and an
   optional "What brings you here?" intent prompt that sends you straight to
   what you came for.
5. **Kind by design.** A tone check nudges before hurtful posts and comments
   ("Pause & rethink"), blocks threats, and offers support resources when
   someone writes about self-harm. The Calm dial lowers heated content in your
   feed. Comment controls, hidden words, block, mute, community notes.
6. **Real life, not just screens.** Plans turn conversations into meetups
   (RSVPs, circles, spaces) and collect shared memories afterwards. Spaces are
   communities with rules and moderators.
7. **Fair to creators.** Tips and supporter memberships with a flat 10%
   platform fee shown at checkout. Supporters-only posts. Posts always reach
   your own followers in their Friends feed; there is no pay-to-reach.
8. **Trust you can see.** Account Status explains every moderation action
   and lets you appeal. Recovery friends get you back in when you are locked
   out. Teen accounts are protected by default.
9. **Your data is yours.** No behavioural ad tracking, no third-party ad SDKs.
   One-tap export of everything; delete means delete. Open-protocol bridging
   (ActivityPub / AT Protocol) is on the roadmap so you can take your
   audience with you.

## 4. Business model

Zemvii must make money without selling attention, or it will drift back to the
incumbents' incentives.

| Stream | How | Guard rails |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Zemvii+** subscription (~$4.99/month) | Custom themes and app icons, longer clips, advanced feed recipes, larger uploads, ad-free Discover | Never paywall safety, privacy, export, or reach |
| **Creator fee** | 10% of tips and memberships | Shown at checkout; creators keep 90% |
| **Contextual sponsored posts** | Matched to the *topic of the feed you are looking at*, never to a profile of you | Discover/Mix only, never in the Friends feed, never to teens or Zemvii+ members, at most 1 in 12 posts, always labelled with "Why this sponsor" |
| **Spaces Pro** | Tools for large communities, clubs and local businesses: events, ticketing, moderation queues | Members are never charged to talk |

Payments run on the web where app-store rules allow, so creators are not taxed
twice.

## 5. How a newcomer can win

Network effects are the incumbents' moat, so Zemvii does not try to replace
everything at once. It wins small groups first.

1. **Wedge: friend groups, not individuals.** Onboarding invites a whole group
   chat at once into a Circle with a shared feed and Plans. A group of five
   friends who move together is worth more than 500 lone sign-ups who find an
   empty app.
2. **Campuses and cities.** Launch in a few universities and cities with Plans
   and Spaces seeded by local clubs, so there is something to *do*, not only
   something to scroll.
3. **Founding creators.** A creator programme with 95% revenue share for the
   first year and guaranteed reach to their followers. Creators bring
   audiences.
4. **Regulation as a tailwind.** Age assurance, teen-safe defaults, finite
   feeds, transparent recommendation and appeals are the direction of law in
   Australia, the EU and US courts [4][5]. Zemvii is compliant by design while
   incumbents retrofit.
5. **Bring your memories.** Import the data-export archives people can already
   download from Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, so switching does not mean
   starting from zero.

### Honest risks

* **Cold start**: mitigated by group onboarding, Plans and Spaces.
* **Lower engagement by design**: we measure meaningful connection, not time
  spent; the business model (subscriptions, creator fees) does not need
  maximum minutes.
* **Moderation cost**: tone nudges and community notes reduce load; human
  review is still required and budgeted.
* **Copycats**: incumbents can copy features but not the incentives; a feed
  that ends hurts an ad-funded business.

## 6. Metrics that matter

* **North star: weekly meaningful connections**, i.e. a two-way exchange
  between people who know each other: a comment answered, a DM thread, a Plan
  RSVP.
* Plans attended per active user per month.
* "Time well spent" survey score (in-app, quarterly, optional).
* Creator median monthly earnings (median, not mean).
* Share of feed sessions that end at "all caught up" rather than abandonment.
* Moderation: appeal overturn rate and time-to-explanation.

We deliberately do **not** optimise daily minutes.
